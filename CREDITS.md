## The VASSAL module

### Version 1 – the original playtest module ("V61")

The original VASSAL module was built for the game's playtest. Its in-module credit reads
*"by Joel Toppen & Frank Esparrago"*. It is the version still listed as **Version 1** on the
[VASSAL module library page](https://vassalengine.org/library/projects/Congress_of_Vienna).

### Version 2 – the rewrite (this repository's current line)

Version 2 is a complete rewrite with a standard board, high-resolution graphics and many
help and convenience features. Its in-module credit reads
*"by Fredrik Lindner & Mark Benson. Published by permission of GMT Games LLC. Game Design by
Frank Esparrago. Original Game Graphics by Terry Leeds."*
